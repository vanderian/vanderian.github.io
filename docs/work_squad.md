#### Soft Skills

#### Summary

At Squad, I was brought in to urgently replace the Twilio framework on Android and continued as the sole Android developer. The codebase was a mix of legacy and modern components, leading to instability and lagging. To ensure feature parity and consistent user experience, I frequently analyzed the iOS codebase, extracting implementation details and adapting iOS-specific logic to the Android platform. This required a deep understanding of both platforms and close collaboration with iOS engineers. Through careful, iterative refactoring, I significantly improved reliability and performance, reduced crashes, and achieved feature parity with iOS. My work enabled the Android app to grow its user base and operate seamlessly with iOS.
